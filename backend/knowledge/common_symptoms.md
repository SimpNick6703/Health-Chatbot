## Common Symptoms Guide

### Fever
A fever is a temporary increase in your body temperature, often due to an illness. Having a fever is a sign that your immune system is fighting off an infection.
- **Normal Body Temperature**: Typically around 98.6°F (37°C), but normal temperature can range from 97°F to 99°F.
- **Common Causes**: Viral infections (like flu or common cold), bacterial infections, heat exhaustion, or certain inflammatory conditions.
- **Self-Care & Comfort**: Rest, stay well-hydrated with water or electrolytes, and wear lightweight clothing. Over-the-counter fever reducers such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen can reduce discomfort when taken according to package instructions.
- **When to Seek Medical Care**: Seek immediate medical care if fever exceeds 103°F (39.4°C), lasts longer than three days, or is accompanied by severe headache, stiff neck, shortness of breath, or confusion.

### Cough
A cough is your body's way of clearing irritants and mucus from your airways.
- **Types**: Dry cough (non-productive) vs. wet/productive cough (produces phlegm/mucus).
- **Common Causes**: Common cold, influenza, bronchitis, asthma, allergies, acid reflux (GERD), or environmental irritants like smoke.
- **Home Care**: Warm liquids (tea with honey for adults), staying hydrated, using a humidifier, and avoiding tobacco smoke.
- **Warning Signs**: Coughing up blood, high fever, difficulty breathing, or a cough persisting for more than 3 weeks requires prompt medical evaluation.

### Headache
Headaches involve pain in the head or upper neck.
- **Types**: Tension headaches (most common, dull aching band around head), migraines (throbbing, often one-sided, sensitivity to light/sound), and sinus headaches (facial pressure around sinuses).
- **Triggers & Care**: Stress, dehydration, lack of sleep, or eye strain. Rest in a dark quiet room, apply cool compresses, and ensure adequate fluid intake.
- **Red Flags**: Sudden severe headache ("thunderclap"), headache following head injury, or accompanied by fever, stiff neck, confusion, or numbness requires emergency medical attention.

### Sore Throat
A sore throat is pain, scratchiness, or irritation of the throat that often worsens when swallowing.
- **Causes**: Most sore throats are caused by viral infections (such as the common cold). Bacterial infections like Strep throat are less common but require antibiotics.
- **Relief Measures**: Warm salt water gargles, warm teas, throat lozenges, and remaining well-hydrated.
