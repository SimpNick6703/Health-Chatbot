## Healthy Lifestyle Guidelines

### Sleep Hygiene
Quality sleep is essential for physical repair, cognitive function, and emotional balance.
- **Recommended Sleep Duration**: Adults generally require 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night.
- **Sleep Hygiene Practices**:
  - Maintain a consistent sleep schedule by going to bed and waking up at the same time daily.
  - Keep the bedroom cool, dark, and quiet.
  - Avoid screens (phones, tablets, TVs) at least 30 to 60 minutes before bedtime.
  - Limit caffeine intake in the late afternoon and evening.

### Physical Activity Guidelines
Regular exercise lowers the risk of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and mood disorders.
- **Adult Recommendations (WHO)**: At least 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise (like brisk walking or cycling) or 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity exercise per week.
- **Strength Training**: Muscle-strengthening exercises involving all major muscle groups twice a week.
- **Sedentary Reduction**: Break up long periods of sitting with standing or short walks every hour.

### Stress Management & Mental Well-being
Chronic stress can negatively impact blood pressure, digestion, and immune function.
- **Effective Techniques**: Deep breathing exercises, mindfulness meditation, regular physical activity, and social connection.
- **Work-Life Balance**: Establish clear boundaries, schedule downtime, and seek professional mental health support when needed.
