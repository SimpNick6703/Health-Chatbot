## Nutrition & Dietary Guidelines

### Balanced Diet Principles
A balanced diet provides essential nutrients required for bodily repair and daily energy without excessive calorie intake.
- **Macronutrients**:
  - **Carbohydrates**: Prioritize complex carbs (whole grains, vegetables, legumes) over refined sugars.
  - **Proteins**: Lean poultry, fish, eggs, tofu, beans, and lentils.
  - **Fats**: Healthy unsaturated fats (olive oil, avocados, nuts, seeds); limit saturated and trans fats.
- **Micronutrients & Fiber**: Aim for 25–30 grams of dietary fiber daily through vegetables, fruits, and whole grains.

### Hydration Guidelines
Water is required for cellular metabolic processes, joint lubrication, and body temperature regulation.
- **Daily Fluid Intake**: General recommendation is about 2 to 3 liters (8 to 12 cups) of fluid per day from beverages and foods, varying based on climate, activity level, and health status.
- **Signs of Dehydration**: Dark yellow urine, dry mouth, dizziness, fatigue, and head aches.

### Dietary Risk Reduction
- **Sodium Limit**: Keep daily sodium intake below 2,000 mg (about 1 teaspoon of salt) to help prevent hypertension.
- **Added Sugars**: Limit added sugars to less than 10% of total daily calories.
