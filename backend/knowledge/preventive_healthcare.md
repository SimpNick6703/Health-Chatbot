## Preventive Healthcare Guide

### Routine Screenings
Preventive health check-ups allow early detection of asymptomatic conditions.
- **Blood Pressure Screening**: Checked at least once every 1–2 years for adults with normal blood pressure, or more frequently if elevated.
- **Lipid Panel (Cholesterol)**: Baseline testing starting in young adulthood, repeated every 4 to 6 years if risk is low.
- **Blood Glucose Testing**: Screening for prediabetes and diabetes starting around age 35, or earlier for individuals with elevated risk factors.
- **Cancer Screenings**: Age-appropriate screenings (e.g., mammograms, cervical pap smears, colorectal cancer screenings such as colonoscopies) as recommended by medical guidelines.

### Vaccination & Immunization
Vaccines stimulate the immune system to protect against infectious diseases.
- **Annual Influenza Vaccine**: Recommended for everyone aged 6 months and older.
- **Tetanus Booster (Tdap/Td)**: Recommended every 10 years for adults.
- **Pneumococcal & Shingles Vaccines**: Recommended for older adults or individuals with specific risk factors.

### Hygiene Practices
- **Hand Hygiene**: Wash hands frequently with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after using the restroom or being in public spaces.
- **Dental Hygiene**: Brush teeth twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss daily to prevent periodontal disease.
